The Committee will administer and manage the fund. Shri Deepak Sood, Whole Time Member of IRDAI, has been appointed as the Ex-Officio Chair, while Shri Rajay Kumar Sinha, Whole Time Member of IRDAI, will serve as a member. Shri B.C Patnaik, Director of the National Insurance Academy, and Shri Sudhir Voleti, Professor at the Indian School of Business, will serve as independent members. The Committee also includes representatives from the Life Insurance Council, General Insurance Council and Council for Insurance Ombudsman. The Executive Director of the Policyholders’ Protection and Grievance Redressal Department will act as the Committee’s convener. The tenure of members listed at serial numbers 3 to 7 will be two years from the date of issuance of the order, extendable by a further two years. The Committee’s functions, conduct of meetings and expenses will be governed by Regulations 8, 9 and 10, respectively, of the Policyholders’ Education and Protection Fund Regulations, 2026. The order has been issued with the approval of the Competent Authority. INSURANCE REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY OF INDIA Ref. No.: IRDAI/PP&GR/ORD/MISC/155/11/2026 | Dated: 25th November, 2026 ORDER Sub: Constitution of Fund Management Committee under IRDAI (Policyholders’ Education and Protection Fund) Regulations 2026 1. Pursuant to Regulation 7(2) of the IRDAI (Policyholders’ Education and Protection Fund) Regulations, 2026, in order to administer and manage the fund, IRDAI constitutes the Fund Management Committee with the following composition: S.No. Name Designation & Organization Role in committee 1 Shri Deepak Sood Whole Time Member, IRDAI Ex-Officio Chair 2 Shri Rajay Kumar Sinha Whole Time Member, IRDAI Member 3 Shri B.C Patnaik Director, National Insurance Academy Member (Independent Member) 4 Shri Sudhir Voleti Professor, Indian School of Business Member (Independent Member) 5 Shri Suresh Parwani Chief (Investment Front Office), LIC of India Member (Representative from Life Insurance Council) 6 Shri Skandan Sivaramakrishnan Consultant, Finance and Taxation, General Insurance Council Member (Representative from General Insurance Council) 7 Shri Hariharasudhan V. Secretary, Council for Insurance Ombudsman Member (Representative from Council for Insurance Ombudsman) 2. Executive Director-Policyholders’ Protection and Grievance Redressal Department shall be the convener of the Committee. 3. The term of office of the members referred to at Sl. Nos. 3 to 7 shall be two years from the date of issuance of this Order, which may be extended by a further period of two years. 4. The functions of the Committee, the conduct of its meetings, and the expenses incurred, shall be governed by regulations 8, 9 and 10, respectively, of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Policyholders’ Education and Protection Fund) Regulations, 2026.
