Tata Sons Listing Row: RBI Reiterates IPO Requirement In Fresh Letter Ahead Of November 26 Board Meetfollow usfollow usAs the tussle over power, control, governance and legacy continues within the Tata Group, the RBI has reiterated its stand for its listing requirement for Tata Sons in a fresh letter. This letter comes just ahead of the scheduled Tata Sons' November 2026 board meeting, where listing is likely to be a crucial point of discussion as per legal and industry experts. In the letter dated November 16, 2026 to the Chief Financial Officer of Tata Sons, the RBI letter states, "while specific guidance has not been sought on any particular regulation/ guideline/ direction, from the discussion in the Board regarding listing, it seems the guidance is perhaps with respect to the same. In this connection, you are advised to comply with all applicable regulations/ guidelines/ directions, issued by the RBI in respect of NBFCs in the Upper Layer (NBFC-UL), as stipulated in extant regulations/ guidelines/ directions hosted on the RBI’s website, including the requirement of listing by your company."The letter concludes by stating, "in case any other specific clarification is required in respect of any regulation/ guideline/ direction issued by the RBI, the same may be communicated to us along with relevant details."This letter is in response to Tata Sons' October 6, 2026 communication seeking guidance from the RBI on compliance with applicable regulations.
