Summary: The choice between self-custody and an exchange wallet determines who controls the private keys required to transfer Bitcoin and who bears responsibility for recovering access after a security incident. In a self-custody arrangement, the owner ordinarily controls the signing credentials; in a custodial exchange account, the service provider generally controls the relevant keys and processes withdrawal requests. Neither model eliminates risk. Self-custody requires protection against lost recovery phrases, compromised devices, phishing and irreversible transfers, while exchange custody introduces counterparty, operational, withdrawal and insolvency risks. Users should distinguish a genuine on-chain withdrawal from an internal exchange ledger transfer, test recovery arrangements without exposing secrets, and maintain transaction records showing addresses, hashes, timestamps, fees and beneficial ownership. Transfers between wallets belonging to the same person should be separately documented from sales, swaps and other transactions that may have tax consequences. Indian virtual digital asset rules require transaction-specific analysis; wallet terminology alone does not determine tax treatment. This guide compares custody models, explains key security decisions, identifies records to preserve and provides a practical framework for choosing an arrangement based on access needs, technical capability and risk tolerance. Bitcoin is controlled on its network through valid cryptographic signatures. A wallet does not ordinarily store coins as physical objects; it manages the credentials needed to authorise transactions. The relevant question is therefore who can sign a transaction and who can prevent or delay one. In self-custody, the user holds or controls the signing credentials. In an exchange account, the exchange generally maintains custody of the on-chain assets and records the customer’s balance on its internal systems. The customer may have a contractual claim and withdrawal facility, but ordinarily does not hold the exchange wallet’s private keys. Self-Custody: Private Keys, Recovery Phrases and Ownership A self-custody wallet can be a software wallet, hardware wallet or more advanced arrangement. The holder must understand how the wallet is backed up, whether it uses a recovery phrase, and how access would be restored after loss or damage to a device. A wallet password and a recovery phrase serve different purposes: a password may protect local access, while the recovery phrase can permit reconstruction of signing credentials. Never share a seed or recovery phrase with support agents, websites, strangers or anyone claiming that verification is required. Anyone obtaining the necessary signing material may be able to move the assets irreversibly. Backup arrangements should account for theft, fire, accidental disposal, unauthorised copying and the possibility that an owner becomes incapacitated. Exchange Wallets: Convenience, Withdrawal Limits and Counterparty Risk An exchange wallet may simplify buying, selling, converting and monitoring assets, but introduces dependence on the provider. Withdrawals can be delayed by security reviews, outages, account freezes, identity verification requirements, liquidity problems or insolvency proceedings. The displayed balance is not, by itself, proof that a customer can withdraw an equivalent amount of Bitcoin immediately. Before relying on an exchange, read its customer agreement, custody arrangements, fee schedule, withdrawal policies, supported networks and dispute process. Consider what records the platform supplies for taxes and whether independent transaction exports are possible. Do not assume crypto balances carry the same statutory protections as insured bank deposits. Actual control and recovery depend on wallet design, multisignature arrangements, account terms and any third-party services used. Protecting Bitcoin Against Phishing and Device Compromise Phishing and malicious software can target both custody models. A fake wallet application may steal recovery material; a fake exchange login page may capture credentials or authentication codes. Verify applications and domains independently, keep operating systems updated, and avoid installing untrusted browser extensions on devices used for signing. For self-custody, consider separating long-term holdings from everyday spending balances and using a suitable hardware signing device where appropriate. Verify the destination address and amount on the trusted signing display before approving a transaction. For exchange accounts, use strong unique passwords, robust multifactor authentication and withdrawal safeguards when available. None of these measures guarantees protection. Testing Bitcoin Wallet Recovery Without Revealing Secrets A backup that has never been tested may fail when needed. Understand the wallet manufacturer’s or software provider’s supported recovery process and consider a controlled test using a separate device or a small-value setup. Avoid entering recovery phrases into unsolicited websites or support forms, and do not photograph them for ordinary cloud storage. Document the existence and location of the recovery procedure without recording the secret itself in a shared operational checklist. Where family succession or business continuity matters, obtain appropriate legal and technical advice on access controls and inheritance arrangements. Do not rely on a single individual knowing the only recovery method. Bitcoin Transfers Between Own Wallets and Exchange Accounts A transfer from an exchange account to a self-custody address may be a withdrawal rather than a sale. A transfer between wallets under the same ownership should be distinguished in the records from a transfer involving a change in beneficial ownership, a sale, a swap or payment for goods or services. The legal and tax character depends on the actual transaction, not the wallet label. Maintain the origin and destination addresses, network, transaction date and time, amount, fees, transaction hash, related account statements and evidence linking both wallets to the same beneficial owner. For a transfer initiated within an exchange, determine whether an on-chain transaction occurred or whether the platform made only an internal ledger entry. Indian Tax Treatment of Bitcoin Wallet Transactions India’s virtual digital asset tax framework includes provisions dealing with income from transfers of VDAs and tax deduction at source in circumstances specified by law. Whether a particular movement amounts to a taxable transfer, whether withholding applies, and how consideration, cost and fees are treated require examination of the transaction and applicable statutory provisions. Do not assume that moving Bitcoin between wallets is taxable merely because it appears as a blockchain transfer, or that a transaction is tax-free merely because an exchange describes it as an internal transfer. Sales, crypto-to-crypto swaps, gifts, rewards and business receipts may involve different considerations. Preserve contemporaneous records and obtain advice where ownership, valuation, cross-border activity or reporting obligations are uncertain. Custody Records, Accounting Controls and Audit Trail For individual investors, keep exchange statements, trade confirmations, wallet transaction exports, transfer hashes, fee details and relevant INR valuation records. Reconcile opening balances, purchases, transfers, disposals and closing balances so that movement between accounts is not double-counted as a new acquisition or sale. Businesses and other entities may need more formal custody and accounting controls, including segregation of duties, access approvals, authorised signers and periodic independent reconciliations. Record who can initiate, approve and execute transfers. Retain evidence of any exceptional access, recovery or wallet migration. Practical Example: Moving Bitcoin From an Exchange to Self-Custody Assume an investor owns 0.05 BTC credited to an exchange account and wishes to move it to a personal wallet. Before withdrawing, the investor verifies the wallet’s recovery arrangements, confirms the receiving address and network, checks the exchange’s withdrawal fee and limits, and considers sending a small test amount. Once the transfer is confirmed, the investor retains the exchange withdrawal record and blockchain transaction hash. The investor then records the movement as a transfer between accounts under the same beneficial ownership, if that is supported by the facts, and separately records any network fee. If Bitcoin is subsequently sold or exchanged, that later transaction should be analysed independently for Indian tax consequences. This is an illustration of recordkeeping, not a determination of tax treatment in every case. Common Bitcoin Custody Mistakes to Avoid Assuming the person who knows an exchange login password controls the on-chain private keys. Keeping the only recovery phrase on a connected device or sharing it with purported support personnel. Sending Bitcoin to an unverified address or assuming a blockchain transaction can be reversed. Ignoring exchange withdrawal limits, outages, fees or contractual restrictions. Failing to document ownership when transferring between personal wallets and exchange accounts. Treating every wallet movement as a sale, or every internal exchange movement as automatically non-taxable. Relying solely on an exchange’s current dashboard instead of preserving transaction exports. Frequently Asked Questions Does self-custody mean Bitcoin is stored inside a hardware wallet? No. Bitcoin remains recorded on its blockchain. The device is used to protect and operate the credentials needed to authorise transactions. Can an exchange stop a Bitcoin withdrawal? A custodial provider may impose restrictions or delays under its systems, terms or applicable law. Review the platform’s withdrawal arrangements before relying on immediate access. What happens if a self-custody recovery phrase is lost? If all methods of recovering the signing credentials are lost, access to the Bitcoin may become permanently impossible. The exact outcome depends on the wallet and any other authorised recovery arrangements. Is transferring Bitcoin between two personal wallets automatically taxable in India? The treatment depends on the legal substance and beneficial ownership of the transaction. Document the transfer and obtain advice where the facts or applicable provisions are unclear. Which records prove a Bitcoin transfer took place? A transaction hash, blockchain details, relevant addresses, exchange withdrawal confirmation and supporting ownership records together provide a stronger audit trail than a screenshot alone. Is an exchange wallet as protected as a bank deposit? Do not assume so. Crypto custody arrangements and customer rights depend on the provider, applicable law and contractual terms; bank-deposit protection should not be presumed. Key Takeaway Self-custody gives users greater direct control over Bitcoin signing credentials but also places recovery and security responsibilities on them. Exchange custody can be convenient but adds dependence on the provider.
