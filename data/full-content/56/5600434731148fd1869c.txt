Articles 2 min read Rupee extends winning run, climbs 17 paise to 93.15 against US dollar The rupee has appreciated by 17 paise, now trading at 93.15 against the US dollar. This positive movement is fueled by robust foreign investments and a weaker dollar. Recovering from a November low of 94.19, the rupee seems poised for further gains. The buying spree from foreign institutional investors in domestic equities bolsters this trend. However, future movements in crude oil and precious metals could influence the rupee's performance. Shivanghi Payal TIMESOFINDIA.COM Published On Nov 20, 2026 at 11:19 AM IST var news_msid = 136015058; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook NEW DELHI: Rupee continued its upward journey for the fifth consecutive session on Friday, gaining 17 paise to trade at 93.15 against the US dollar in early deals. Strong foreign investor buying in Indian equities and a decline in the dollar against major global currencies helped the currency hold its ground, even as crude oil prices moved higher and stock markets remained under pressure.The currency began the session at 93.25 at the interbank foreign exchange market before strengthening to 93.15. Its latest gains follow a 9-paise rise on Thursday, when it closed at 93.32 against the US dollar. Advt Rupee has gained 78 paise, or 0.83%, over the three sessions preceding Thursday, recovering from its November 13 closing level of 94.19.The movement in crude oil, gold and silver prices could influence rupee's trajectory in the coming sessions. Anuj Choudhary from Mirae Asset Sharekhan expects the domestic currency to retain a slightly positive bias, with lower crude oil prices and a sharp correction in precious metal prices potentially easing the country's import bill."Softening of US Treasury yields may also support the rupee. However, a strong dollar and hawkish Fed speak may cap sharp gains. USD-INR spot price is expected to trade in a range of Rs 92.85 to Rs 93.40," he added.The dollar index, which tracks the US currency against six major currencies, slipped 0.07% to 103.76. Brent crude, on the other hand, edged 0.40% higher to $92.35 per barrel in futures trade.Foreign fund inflows continued to provide support to the rupee. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) purchased domestic equities worth Rs 7,411.41 crore on Thursday, according to exchange data.The buying came even as domestic benchmarks started Friday's session in the red. BSE Sensex was down 217.03 points, or 0.26%, at 82,804.72, while the Nifty shed 58.70 points, or 0.23%, to trade at 25,455.60 in early deals. However, Dalal Street turned green later in the session.
