This development, showcased at the IIT Madras Energy Summit 2026, marks a significant milestone in the advancement of indigenous zinc-based energy storage solutions and highlights the potential of zinc in diversifying energy storage for electric mobility. The prototype, integrated with a 1 kWh zinc-air battery, was unveiled in the presence of prominent figures including Shri Praveen Chakravarthy, Member of Parliament, and Mr. Amarendu Prakash, CEO of Hindustan Zinc Limited. The zinc-air battery serves as a complementary energy source to the traditional lithium-ion battery, providing a steady supply of energy and contributing nearly 10% of the vehicle’s total energy requirements. This dual-battery configuration showcases the potential of zinc-based technologies to enhance existing energy storage systems, particularly in the context of electric mobility. The collaboration between Hindustan Zinc and IIT Madras underscores the growing demand for diversified, sustainable energy storage solutions amidst the global shift towards clean energy. Zinc, with its abundance, recyclability, and cost advantages, presents a promising alternative for next-generation battery technologies. India’s rich zinc resources offer a strategic opportunity to bolster indigenous capabilities in energy storage, supporting the nation’s journey towards energy self-reliance. Mr. Amarendu Prakash expressed Hindustan Zinc’s commitment to exploring new applications for zinc, emphasising the company’s dedication to translating scientific research into practical innovations. The collaboration with IIT Madras aligns with Hindustan Zinc’s broader strategy to explore emerging applications of zinc and expand its presence in value-added product segments. As part of its HZL 2.0 growth strategy, the company continues to drive innovation and strengthen zinc’s role as a critical enabler in the global energy transition. Industry analysts view this collaboration as a positive step towards enhancing India’s capabilities in energy storage technologies. The successful demonstration of the zinc-air battery at a vehicle level signifies a promising future for zinc-based energy solutions, potentially reducing reliance on traditional lithium-ion batteries and contributing to a more sustainable and diversified energy landscape.
