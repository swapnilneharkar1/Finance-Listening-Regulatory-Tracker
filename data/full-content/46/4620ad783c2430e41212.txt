This approval marks a significant milestone for Lupin as it continues to expand its presence in the U.S. pharmaceutical market. The Ferric Citrate Tablets, 210 mg, are bioequivalent to Auryxia® Tablets, 210 mg, originally developed by Keryx Biopharmaceuticals Inc. These tablets are indicated for use in adults with chronic kidney disease (CKD) for managing hyperphosphatemia in patients undergoing dialysis and treating iron deficiency anaemia in those not on dialysis. The reference drug, Auryxia®, has reported estimated annual sales of USD 58.1 million in the U.S. as of September 2026, according to IQVIA MAT data. Lupin Limited, renowned for its extensive pharmaceutical portfolio, operates across more than 100 markets worldwide. The company is a leader in the development of generics, complex generics, specialty medicines, and biosimilars, with a robust presence in the U.S. and Indian markets. Its operations are supported by 15 manufacturing facilities and six research centres, employing over 26,000 professionals globally. The company’s strategic focus on sustainability, digital health, and patient-support initiatives underscores its commitment to enhancing the healthcare ecosystem. The approval of Ferric Citrate Tablets by the U.S. FDA is expected to strengthen Lupin’s market position in the U.S., particularly in the therapeutic areas of cardiovascular, respiratory, diabetes, gastrointestinal, and women’s health. Analysts view this development as a positive step for Lupin, potentially enhancing its revenue streams and market share in the competitive U.S. pharmaceutical landscape.
