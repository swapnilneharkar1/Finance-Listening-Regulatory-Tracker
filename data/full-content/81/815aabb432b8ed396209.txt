Advt Highlighting primary market activity, Pandey said 235 companies have collectively raised around Rs 1.5 lakh crore through initial public offerings (IPOs) so far in FY27, underscoring robust investor appetite for new listings.According to him, corporate bonds continue to play a crucial role in diversifying financing sources for businesses and reducing dependence on traditional bank lending.Outstanding corporate bonds stood at approximately Rs 62 lakh crore as of August 2026 which is nearly 55 per cent of the bank credit extended to industry and services sectors, adds Pandey.Companies have raised more than Rs 5 lakh crore through bond issuances during the current financial year so far, he said.The SEBI chief also highlighted the growing role of Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) in providing risk capital to emerging enterprises and businesses that may not yet be ready to access public markets.He said cumulative investments by AIFs had reached around Rs 7.5 lakh crore by September 2026, while committed capital exceeded Rs 18 lakh crore.On infrastructure financing and capital recycling, Pandey said Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) have enabled developers to monetise operational assets and redeploy capital into new projects while offering investors access to income-generating assets. Assets managed by REITs and InvITs have grown to more than Rs 10 lakh crore, according to him. Advt He said India's capital markets today cater to a wide spectrum of financing requirements, ranging from corporate expansion and venture funding to infrastructure development and long-term capital formation.Pandey also stated that continued economic growth, broader investor participation and policy reforms have strengthened the capital markets ecosystem and positioned it as a key pillar supporting India's investment and development agenda.--IANSag/ IANS Published On Nov 20, 2026 at 12:45 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
