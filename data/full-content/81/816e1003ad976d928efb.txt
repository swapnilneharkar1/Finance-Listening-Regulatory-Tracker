The bank expects operating expense ratios to remain sticky in the near term.The bank reported a net profit of Rs1,390 crore, up 45% year-on-year but down 18% quarter-on-quarter, beating estimates by 10%. Higher other income and lower-than-expected provisions supported earnings, partly offset by higher operating expenses, including a one-time adverse impact of Rs33 crore from a change in ESOP accounting, according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. Advt Federal Bank's other income rose 38% year-on-year and 42% quarter-on-quarter to Rs1,490 crore, supported by treasury income of Rs238 crore from the encashment of SR investments and robust IPO activity. Operating profit before provisions and tax increased 35% year-on-year and 17% quarter-on-quarter to Rs2,230 crore. The cost-to-income ratio stood at 52.1%.Net interest income grew 26% year-on-year and 7% quarter-on-quarter to Rs3,150 crore. Net interest margin contracted by one basis point sequentially to 3.32%, as a four-basis-point reduction in the cost of funds was largely offset by a three-basis-point decline in yield on advances.NIM expansionManagement reiterated its guidance for a five to six basis-point expansion in NIM each quarter, while cautioning that the improvement would not be linear. It also expects return on assets to improve by three to four basis points each quarter, although the improvement is not expected to be linear.Advances grew 22% year-on-year and 7.5% quarter-on-quarter to Rs2.98 lakh crore, led by small and medium enterprises, gold loans and corporate lending. Deposits increased 18.6% year-on-year and 7.1% quarter-on-quarter to Rs3.42 lakh crore, aided by Rs14,900 crore in FCNR(B) deposit mobilisation. The CASA mix declined by 19 basis points sequentially to 32%. Advt Asset quality improved, with gross non-performing assets declining by 20 basis points sequentially to 1.32% and net non-performing assets falling by one basis point to 0.17%. Provisions declined 6% year-on-year but rose 8% quarter-on-quarter to Rs340 crore, while slippages increased 3% sequentially to Rs426 crore.
