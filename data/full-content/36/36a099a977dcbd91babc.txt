Articles 2 min read NBFC-MFIs increase microfinance market share to 44.7% as industry portfolio declines 5%: Report According to a CRIF report, NBFC-MFIs reported a portfolio outstanding of ₹1.46 lakh crore in September 2026, while the overall microfinance gross loan portfolio stood at ₹3.28 lakh crore. Disbursements rose 5.1% year-on-year to ₹56,956 crore in Q2 FY27. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 5, 2026 at 02:11 PM IST var news_msid = 135565930; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook NBFC-MFIs strengthened their position in India's microfinance market, accounting for 44.7% of the industry's portfolio outstanding as of September 2026, compared with 38.8% a year earlier, according to CRIF's latest report.The outstanding portfolio of NBFC-MFIs stood at ₹1.46 lakh crore, up 9.4% year-on-year and 0.2% quarter-on-quarter. In comparison, the overall microfinance gross loan portfolio (GLP) declined 5% year-on-year and 1.4% quarter-on-quarter to ₹3.28 lakh crore.Industry portfolio and exposureThe industry portfolio comprised 9.6 crore active loans, serving 6.4 crore borrowers as of September 2026. Average exposure per loan increased to ₹34,200, up 5.8% quarter-on-quarter and 22.7% year-on-year. Advt Disbursements and ticket sizeMicrofinance disbursements stood at ₹56,956 crore in Q2 FY27, declining 7.5% sequentially but increasing 5.1% year-on-year. The average microfinance ticket size rose to ₹63,100 during the quarter, up 1.6% quarter-on-quarter and 17.5% year-on-year.The ₹50,000-₹80,000 segment accounted for 41.3% of origination value in Q2 FY27. The share of loans in the ₹80,000-₹1 lakh segment rose to 18.5% from 14.7% a year earlier, while loans above ₹1 lakh accounted for 15.1%, up from 11.2% in Q2 FY26. Disbursement value in the above-₹1 lakh segment grew 41.3% year-on-year, as per the report.Lender-wise originationsNBFC-MFIs accounted for 49% of the total disbursement value in Q2 FY27, compared with 47.7% in the corresponding quarter last year. Their disbursement value grew 8.9% year-on-year. Banks, NBFCs and small finance banks accounted for 14.2%, 14% and 22.9%, respectively.Asset quality and borrower profileOverall microfinance loan delinquency remained stable in September 2026, with PAR 1-30, PAR 31-90 and PAR 91-180 each below 1%. PAR 31-90 and PAR 91-180 remained at 0.8%, while PAR 31-180 stood at 1.6%, compared with 4.6% in September 2025.Borrowers associated with two or fewer active lenders constituted 81.2% of the portfolio. Their PAR 31-180 stood at 1.9% in September 2026, compared with 4.1% for borrowers associated with three lenders and 6.6% for those associated with more than three lenders. Advt State-wise portfolioBihar remained the largest microfinance market, with a portfolio outstanding of ₹51,100 crore. Tamil Nadu recorded a 3.7% quarter-on-quarter increase in portfolio outstanding to ₹40,300 crore, while its PAR 31-180 improved to 1.4% in September 2026 from 1.5% in June 2026.The top 10 states accounted for 82.3% of the industry's overall gross loan portfolio, as stated in the report.
