The amendments extend SCOMET authorisation-based facilitation under Stock and Sale, General Authorisation for Export after Repair in India (GAER), General Authorisation for Export of Telecommunication (GAET) and General Authorisation for Export of Information Security Items (GAEIS) to eligible exporters holding valid 4-Star or 5-Star Status Holder Certificates, subject to other prescribed conditions. The amendments permit eligible 4-Star and 5-Star Status Holders to furnish their valid status certificates as alternatives to specified Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) certifications. The changes cover paragraphs 10.10(c)(vii), 10.12(D)(A)(m), 10.12(D)(B)(5), 10.15(I)(1)(b)(vi) and 10.15(II)(1)(b)(vi) of the HBP 2023. Further, the validity of GAER authorisations for export of imported SCOMET items after repair in India has been increased from one year to three years from the date of issue. The amended reporting requirement provides for submission of prescribed post-shipment reports within 30 days from the end of each quarter, replacing the earlier requirement of reporting within 30 days from the date of export. The amendments aim to facilitate exports of repaired SCOMET items while retaining the applicable authorisation conditions and reporting obligations. Government of India Ministry of Commerce & Industry Directorate General of Foreign Trade Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi Public Notice No. 36/2026-27-DGFT | Dated 2 November 2026 Subject: Amendment to Chapter 10 of the Handbook of Procedures (HBP), 2023 – Extension of SCOMET Authorisation-based facilitation to eligible 4-Star and 5-Star Status Holders and extension of validity of General Authorisation for Export after Repair in India (GAER) – reg. In exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby makes the following amendments to Chapter 10 of the Handbook of Procedures (HBP) 2023, with immediate effect. 2. S.No.4 of the Table under Para 10.17 GAER issued for export of imported SCOMET items after repair shall be valid for a period of one year from the date of issue of GAER, subject to subsequent post reporting(s) within 30 days from the date of such export. GAER issued for export of imported SCOMET items after repair shall be valid for a period of three years from the date of issue of GAER, subject to submission subsequent of subsequent post shipment reports within 30 days from the end of each quarter. 3. Effect of this Public Notice: This Public Notice extends the existing SCOMET Authorisation-based facilitation under the Stock and Sale, General Authorisation for Export after Repair (GAER), General Authorisation for Export of Telecommunication (GAET) and General Authorisation for Export of Information Security Items (GAEIS) procedures to eligible exporters holding valid 4-Star or 5-Star Status Holder Certificates, subject to fulfilment of the other prescribed conditions. It also extends the validity of GAER from one year to three years, subject to compliance with the prescribed post-reporting requirements and other applicable conditions, with a view to enhancing ease of doing business and facilitating exports of SCOMET items after repair in India.
