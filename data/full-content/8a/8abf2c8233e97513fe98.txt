However, proposed changes to insurance distribution commission guidelines could significantly affect revenue if implemented in their current form.The company plans to improve distribution and productivity through multi product Sampoorna branches, Hercules led cross selling, digital acquisition capabilities and its payments platform. Management expects to offset the potential impact of regulatory changes through revenue diversification, including cross selling, and adjustments to its business model, according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. Advt Gold loans are expected to remain a key growth driver, with L&T Finance targeting a 14 to 15% share of AUM, equivalent to Rs 40,000 crore to Rs 45,000 crore, by FY31. Its gold loan book grew 242% year on year in the second quarter of FY27. Growth in loans against property, personal loans, small and medium enterprise lending and affordable housing is expected to provide additional avenues for expansion.The company's total loan book grew 28% year on year and 5.4% quarter on quarter to around Rs1.37 lakh crore in the September quarter. Disbursements increased 27% year on year to Rs24,000 crore. Two wheeler finance disbursements rose 26% to Rs 3,170 crore, gold finance disbursements surged 229% to Rs 3,240 crore, and personal loan disbursements increased 45% to Rs 4,230 crore.Retail loans grew around 29% year on year, led by personal and gold loans. Personal loans grew 72%, rural business loans increased 22% and SME loans rose 27%. Wholesale loans remained broadly stable at around Rs 2,000 crore.L&T Finance reported a 32% year on year increase in profit after tax to Rs 970 crore in the quarter. Net interest income rose around 33% to Rs 3,190 crore, while profit before provisions and tax increased 31% to Rs 2,150 crore. Operating expenses grew around 28% to Rs 1,370 crore, and the cost to income ratio increased around 20 basis points sequentially to 38.9%.Margins stable Advt Net interest margin plus fee income remained stable sequentially at around 10.5%, supported by lower borrowing costs and proactive liability management. The reported cost of borrowings declined 25 basis points sequentially to 6.95%, as favourable liquidity conditions and lower market rates during July and August enabled the company to refinance higher cost borrowings and raise fresh funds at more attractive rates. The lower borrowing cost base provides a cushion against anticipated hardening of interest rates.Asset quality improved during the quarter, with the consolidated gross stage three ratio declining 10 basis points sequentially to 2.75% and the net stage three ratio falling five basis points to 0.85%. L&T Finance expects credit costs to moderate to 2 to 2.2% by the fourth quarter of FY27, supported by improving credit metrics, disciplined underwriting, AI led collection efficiencies, calibrated rural disbursements and insurance backed portfolio protection. Advt The brokerage estimates L&T Finance's loan book to grow at a compound annual rate of around 24% and profit after tax at 28% over FY26 to FY28. It expects consolidated return on assets and return on equity to reach 2.6% and 15%, respectively, in FY28, and reiterates a buy rating with a target price of Rs330, based on 2.2 times estimated book value per share for September 2028.
