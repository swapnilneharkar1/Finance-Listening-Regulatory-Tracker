The latest available figures are for the week through November 6. Advt "Stress on the rupee has abated on both fronts -- the current account, with lower oil prices, and the capital account, with the return of portfolio inflows," said Vivek Kumar, an economist at QuantEco Research."The shift in these dynamics has allowed the RBI to step back."The moderation in dollar sales follows a prolonged period of heavy intervention, with the RBI estimated to have deployed $80 billion through spot dollar sales and dollar-rupee swaps since June 8, when its special measures to attract foreign-currency inflows took effect."The RBI's reduced need to intervene reflects the combined impact of its efforts to rein in hedging demand and a revival in foreign portfolio flows," said Sengupta.A significant reversal in foreign investor sentiment has brought nearly $12 billion into Indian equities so far this month. Foreign investors have also invested over $1 billion in Indian debt over the same period.The rupee is Asia's best-performing currency this month, up 1.5%. It has recovered 3.5% from its year-to-date low to trade at 93.50 per US dollar.RBI's NEXT MOVECentral bank intervention has drawn down India's foreign exchange reserves by about $66 billion from their September peak of $785.7 billion. The fall incorporates valuation changes in the RBI's foreign currency assets.Market participants are watching for levels at which the central bank may step in to buy dollars, both to replenish reserves and manage its sizeable forward book, which stood at around $240 billion in September. Advt "The improvement in external conditions and the rupee's rally offer the RBI an opportunity to rebuild reserves," Kumar said.Its sizeable forward book provides another reason to buy dollars when market conditions are favourable, he added.Beyond rebuilding its reserves, market participants are on the lookout for any indication of when the RBI might unwind currency-supporting measures relating to shifting dollar demand from oil companies away from the spot market and making hedging of large transactions more expensive.(Reporting by Nimesh Vora; Editing by Ronojoy Mazumdar) Reuters Published On Nov 19, 2026 at 02:00 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
