Articles 2 min read India's economic pillars show 'no fragility', says Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman highlighted the government's commitment to increasing capital expenditure to promote economic growth. She remarked on the importance of fiscal prudence while implementing spending strategies effectively. Sitharaman emphasized the need for public investment to enhance welfare programs and improve access to essential services. The government aims to provide comprehensive insurance coverage by 2047 to foster financial inclusion.
