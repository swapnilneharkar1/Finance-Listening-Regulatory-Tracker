Articles 1 min read ADB Signs $75 Million Loan Deal with Veritas Finance to Expand MSME Lending in India The funding will help Veritas Finance expand access to formal credit for underserved businesses in rural and semi-urban India, with a dedicated loan product for women borrowers. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 27, 2026 at 03:52 PM IST var news_msid = 136217915; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Asian Development Bank (ADB) has signed a $75 million loan agreement with Veritas Finance Limited (VFL) to expand lending to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) in India, including at least 30% for eligible women borrowers.The project has also mobilised $55 million in parallel loans from commercial lenders and includes technical support to develop a dedicated secured term loan product for women. It aims to address India's MSME financing gap of more than $300 billion.The transaction marks Veritas Finance's first external commercial borrowing and syndicated US dollar loan, potentially opening access to offshore funding markets. Advt "This collaboration with ADB marks an important milestone in our next phase of growth and reinforces our commitment to creating a meaningful and lasting impact among the communities we serve," said VFL Chief Executive Officer D. Arulmany.Established in 2015, Veritas Finance is an RBI-registered NBFC operating across semi-urban and rural India. As of September 2026, it had a loan portfolio of approximately $1.1 billion, with a presence in more than 500 locations across ten states.
