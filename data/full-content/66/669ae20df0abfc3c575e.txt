Summary: The article provides a comprehensive guide to reporting exempt, nil-rated and non-taxable supplies in Form GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26, focusing on Tables 5, 10 and 11. It explains the classification and reporting of B2B and B2C transactions, exports, supplies to Special Economic Zones (SEZs), supplies under the Letter of Undertaking (LUT) mechanism and transactions attracting the reverse charge mechanism (RCM). Detailed tables identify the appropriate reporting fields for bills of supply, invoices, credit notes, debit notes and subsequent amendments. The author discusses the option of reporting net figures in Tables 5A to 5F instead of separately reporting adjustments in Tables 5H to 5K. Through 19 practical scenarios involving an actual exempt turnover of ₹10 crore, the article illustrates the treatment of differences between turnover reported in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B during the relevant financial year and the subsequent specified period. Particular emphasis is placed on reporting actual turnover in the annual return and identifying transactions requiring disclosure in Tables 10 and 11. The article also examines the treatment of advances, reverse charge supplies, commercial credit notes and amendments to B2B and B2C transactions. Practical examples explain how increases and decreases in turnover should be reflected in Tables 5J and 5K, including amendments to previously reported credit notes. The article serves as a practical reference for GST professionals and taxpayers preparing annual returns for FY 2025-26.
