6 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(i)] 1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Listing of equity instruments in permissible jurisdictions) Amendment Rules, 2026. (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 2. Throughout the Companies (Listing of equity shares in permissible jurisdictions) Rules, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the principal rules), for the word “shares”, wherever it occurs, the word “instruments’ shall be substituted. 3. In the principal rules, in sub-rule (1) of rule 2, after clause (b), the following clause shall be inserted, namely: - ’(ba) “equity instruments” means equity shares, convertible debentures, preference shares issued by an Indian Company;’ 4. In the principal rules, in rule 3,- (i) in clause (a), after the word, “companies”, the words, “other than public companies in the International Financial Services Centres jurisdiction”, shall be inserted; (ii) after clause (b), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:– “(c) unlisted public companies in the International Financial Services Centres jurisdiction, in so far as they are in accordance with the provisions of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (50 of 2019) and with the regulations framed thereunder.”. 5. In the principal rules, in rule 4, for the words “existing shareholders” occurring at both the places, the words “existing holders” shall be substituted. 6. In the principal rules, in rule 5, (i) in clause (d), after the words “has a negative net worth” the following proviso, shall be inserted, namely: - “Provided that a company having a negative net worth shall be eligible to issue its equity instruments for listing, if the issuer undertakes to allot at least seventy-five per cent. of the net offer to qualified institutional buyers in accordance with the regulations framed in this regard by the respective securities market regulator in permissible jurisdiction for purpose of this rule.”. (ii) in clause (e), in the proviso, for the words “two years”, the words, “more than six months” shall be substituted; (iii) in clause (g), after the words “specified period”, the words “and such default has not been made good” shall be inserted; 7.
