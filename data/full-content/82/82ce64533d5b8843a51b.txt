This approval enables the treatment of HER2-positive breast cancer across various stages, marking a significant milestone in Biocon’s strategic partnership with Brazilian biotechnology company Bionovis and public pharmaceutical laboratory Bahiafarma. This development stems from a landmark 10-year Productive Development Partnership (PDP) agreement, which allows the consortium to supply locally manufactured biosimilar Pertuzumab to Brazil’s public healthcare sector. This initiative is expected to enhance accessibility to cancer treatment and generate substantial savings for the healthcare system. The approval in Brazil follows recent endorsements by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the European Commission, underscoring Biocon’s robust regulatory and scientific capabilities. Susheel Umesh, Biocon’s Chief Commercial Officer for Emerging Markets, highlighted the significance of this approval. He noted that it not only strengthens Biocon’s global oncology portfolio but also advances the company’s PDP partnership with Bionovis and Bahiafarma. This collaboration aims to provide affordable, high-quality biologic therapy to Brazilian patients battling HER2-positive breast cancer. Hertuzo® is a biosimilar to the reference medicine Perjeta® and is approved as a 420 mg concentrate for infusion. The approval was based on comprehensive comparability assessments, demonstrating its biosimilarity to the reference product. Bionovis, known for its focus on R&D and manufacturing of complex biologics, and Bahiafarma, a key player in pharmaceutical research and development in Brazil, are pivotal partners in this venture. Biocon, a global leader in biopharmaceuticals, is committed to offering affordable, life-changing medicines worldwide. With a diverse portfolio that includes biosimilars and generics, the company addresses critical healthcare challenges, particularly in oncology. Analysts suggest that this approval could further solidify Biocon’s presence in the Latin American market, enhancing its reputation as a pioneer in the biosimilars landscape.
