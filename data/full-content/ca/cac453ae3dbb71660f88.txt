Articles 2 min read Gold loan AUM growth for NBFCs to moderate to 40 pc in FY27: Crisil PTI Published On Nov 23, 2026 at 07:38 PM IST var news_msid = 136105693; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai, The growth in assets under management (AUM) for non-bank finance companies (NBFCs) is set to moderate to 40 per cent in FY27 from the 78 per cent in the fiscal year before, a rating agency said on Monday.Base effect and a sluggish demand environment were cited as the primary reasons for the slowdown in the growth by Crisil Ratings.Its Chief Rating Officer Krishnan Sitaraman, however, said the vertical continues to be one of the best asset classes for the NBFCs from a risk-reward perspective given the very low losses in the segment because of the underlying security.There is a shift in the profile of the borrower, where borrowing against gold is not being seen as a means of last resort or as stigma, he said, adding that promoters of small firms borrow against gold for meeting their working capital requirements. Advt About the correction in gold prices, he said it is an important monitorable but added that a sensitivity analysis makes the agency believe that a sharp fall of over 15 per cent is a rarity.He added that the agency does not see a surge in credit costs because of the gold price correction.The depressed prices was also highlighted as being among the factors responsible for slower growth by the agency, but an official stressed that the number of borrowers in the segment and also the tonnage is growing.The asset quality uptick being witnessed in the industry is largely due to change in regulatory norms and will not lead to loan losses, he said.The agency said that the overall NBFC AUM is set to grow at 20 per cent levels in FY27 and FY28.Consumption demand remains resilient and supportive of credit growth. At the same time, relatively low credit penetration in several borrower segments, widening reach enabled by technology, and strong lender balance sheets provide a foundation for sustained growth, it said.However, it added that the impact of deficient monsoon, inflationary pressures, interest-rate movements and geopolitical uncertainties remain monitorable."Faster growth in gold loans, unsecured lending and loan against property will contrast with relatively steady growth in the traditional home loan and vehicle finance segments," Sitaraman said. PTI Advt Gold loans’ share of new-to-credit accounts rose to 24.9% in the September quarter from 16.4% a year earlier, while consumer durable loans remained the largest entry point into formal credit at 30.7%, according to a CRIF report.
