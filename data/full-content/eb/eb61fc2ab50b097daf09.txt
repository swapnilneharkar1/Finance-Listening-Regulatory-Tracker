After hearing learned counsel and examining the material placed on record, the Supreme Court allowed applications seeking exemption from filing a certified copy of the impugned order and official translations of annexures. It issued notice, granted liberty to serve the Standing Counsel for the State of Punjab and directed that the petitioner would enjoy interim protection against arrest subject to cooperation with the investigating agency. The supplied Supreme Court order is an interim procedural order and does not contain a final determination of anticipatory bail or detailed findings concerning the statutory conditions for bail under Section 37 of the NDPS Act. The proceedings originated from FIR No. 124 dated 21 October 2022, registered at Police Station Shimlapuri, Ludhiana, Punjab, for an offence under Section 21 of the NDPS Act. The petitioner had approached the Punjab and Haryana High Court by filing his first application for anticipatory bail under Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973. By an order dated 2 December 2022, the High Court issued notice of motion and granted interim protection to the petitioner. The protection was granted during the pendency of the anticipatory bail proceedings and was not a final order allowing bail. During the subsequent hearing before the High Court, the State counsel, acting on instructions from ASI Bachhitar Singh, informed the Court that the petitioner had joined the investigation. However, the State opposed confirmation of anticipatory bail on the ground that the petitioner had allegedly misused the concession of interim protection and engaged in further unlawful activities. In particular, the State submitted that the petitioner had been named as an accused in FIR No. 36 dated 28 March 2023, registered under the NDPS Act at the same Police Station, Shimlapuri, Ludhiana. The State further informed the High Court that the petitioner had been arrested in connection with the subsequent FIR. FULL TEXT OF THE SUPREME COURT ORDER 1. Heard learned counsel appearing for petitioner and perused the material placed on record. 2. Applications seeking exemption from filing certified copy of the impugned order as also for seeking exemption from filing official translation of the Annexures are allowed. 3. Issue notice. 4. Liberty is granted to serve the Standing counsel for the State of Punjab. 5. In the meanwhile, interim protection against arrest is granted to the petitioner, subject to his joining and cooperating with the investigation as and when called upon do so.
