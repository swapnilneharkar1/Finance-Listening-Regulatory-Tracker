Articles 2 min read Latest FD interest rates after recent rate changes: ICICI Bank, Indian Bank, SBI and HDFC Bank Several banks including ICICI Bank and Indian Bank have increased their fixed deposit interest rates recently. The Reserve Bank of India raised the repo rate, influencing banks to adjust their deposit rates. ICICI Bank now offers 6.60% for general citizens and 7.20% for senior citizens on select tenures. Indian Bank has also raised rates on specific fixed deposit options for its customers. The possibility of further FD rate hikes can’t also be ruled out.However, banks may not increase rates on all of their FD tenures and they may also take from some days to years to increase the rates. The FD rate hike may also vary from bank to bank.Here we take you through the recent FD rate changes by ICICI Bank and Bank of India. We will also take you through the FD rates offered by State Bank of India (SBI) and HDFC Bank. The same revised rate applies to deposits with tenures of five years and one day to 10 years and to the five-year tax-saver FD.Senior citizens can earn up to 7.20% on deposits with tenures of three years and one day to five years and on the five-year tax-saver FD.Latest Indian Bank FD interest ratesIndian Bank has increased the interest rate on its Ind Grow (555 days) FD from 6.65% to 6.80% and on its Ind Prosper (777 days) FD from 6.60% to 6.75%, effective October 23, 2026, for deposits below Rs 3 crore.
