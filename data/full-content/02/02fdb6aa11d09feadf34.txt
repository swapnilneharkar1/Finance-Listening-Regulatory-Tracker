Articles 2 min read Parliamentary Committee on Finance to examine 46 economic issues, including crypto, AI, in FY'27 Parliament's Standing Committee on Finance has identified 46 subjects for detailed examination this fiscal year. Topics include artificial intelligence in finance, virtual digital assets, and currency management among others. PTI Published On Oct 26, 2026 at 09:58 AM IST var news_msid = 135202748; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook BJP MP Bhartruhari MahtabParliament's Standing Committee on Finance has selected 46 subjects, including artificial intelligence in finance, currency management, and a study on virtual digital assets, for detailed examination and report preparation during the current fiscal year.Earlier this month, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla had reconstituted several Parliamentary Committees related to different Ministries.BJP MP Bhartruhari Mahtab was reappointed as Chairperson of the Standing Committee on Finance, which comprises 21 members from the Lok Sabha and 10 members of the Rajya Sabha.According to a Lok Sabha bulletin, the Standing Committee on Finance (2026-27) has selected 46 subjects relating to the Ministry of Finance (Departments of Economic Affairs, Expenditure, Public Enterprises and Investment & Public Asset Management, Revenue and Financial Services); the Ministry of Corporate Affairs; NITI Aayog; and the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation for detailed examination and report during the year. Advt The subjects selected by the committee are pertinent in the prevailing economic scenarios. These include strategy for price and currency stability, currency management, regulatory mechanism in the financial sector, and monetisation of public sector assets.The Committee would also study banking, pension, and health insurance sectors, social security for the informal sector, and evaluate various central government schemes.It would review the evolving macroeconomic landscape, small savings schemes, RBI's role in the evolving economy, and a performance review of SEBI.The panel would also look at customs, GST, and direct tax reforms, conduct a study on virtual digital assets and the way forward, analyse emerging issues and prospects of artificial intelligence in the financial sector, and regulatory gaps in the NBFC sector.With regard to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, the Standing Committee would study challenges and the way forward for the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), review CSR and corporate e-governance, and the accountability of auditors.The committee would also look at the role of NITI Aayog in the formulation of a sectoral roadmap and the functioning of the aspirational districts programme.program.For the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), the panel would study national accounts statistics and suggest ways to strengthen the economic data mechanism.
