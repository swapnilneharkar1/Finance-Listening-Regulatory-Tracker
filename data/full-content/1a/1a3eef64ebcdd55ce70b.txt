Articles 1 min read Shift in pension and insurance money to equities dampens long-term G-sec demand: RBI DG The demand for long-term government securities has seen a marginal decline recently. Insurance companies and pension funds are increasingly focusing on equities instead of sovereign debt. This change has impacted the stability of the government securities market and highlighted challenges in liquidity distribution. The Reserve Bank of India emphasizes the need for a coordinated approach and broader participation from institutional investors. PTI Published On Nov 24, 2026 at 06:21 PM IST var news_msid = 136131209; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The demand for long-term government securities has declined marginally as insurance companies and pension funds have shifted their exposure to equities, Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) Deputy Governor Rohit Jain has said."It has been observed that demand from banks, insurers and pension funds for long-term sovereign debt has marginally declined, with insurance and pension funds shifting towards more equity exposure. This has structurally reduced such demand for Government Securities," Jain said while addressing the Primary Dealers' Association of India (PDAI) Seminar 2026 on Monday.The RBI published the speech on Tuesday. Advt Jain said historically, insurance companies and pension funds provided the most consistent demand for longer-dated paper.However, Jain said that in an uncertain environment, issuing longer-dated securities at attractive yields becomes more challenging as market participants become hesitant to take duration risk.He added that asset managers and other investors are increasingly selective regarding duration, liquidity and mark-to-market risk.Further, Jain said that government securities market stability and resilience will require a more coordinated approach, and India needs to continue the focus on credible fiscal and debt-management policies, a transparent and predictable auction calendar, deeper secondary-market liquidity, and broader participation from long-term institutional investors.Jain said improving the distribution of liquidity across the yield curve remained a challenge and called on primary dealers to increase market-making activities, particularly by providing two-way quotes in off-the-run securities.He also flagged weak secondary market activity in State Government Securities, which accounted for only about 5 per cent of total secondary market turnover in the first half of FY27, despite an increase in state government borrowings over the years.
