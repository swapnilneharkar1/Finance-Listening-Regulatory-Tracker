Gross collections increased 14.02% to ₹17,50,285.89 crore from ₹15,35,076.31 crore, while refunds increased 25.91% to ₹3,08,306.98 crore from ₹2,44,872.21 crore. Gross corporate tax collections rose from ₹6,91,010.61 crore to ₹8,01,471.71 crore, and net corporate tax collections rose from ₹5,36,285.79 crore to ₹6,31,959.53 crore. Gross non-corporate tax collections rose from ₹8,08,049.19 crore to ₹8,94,716.18 crore, and net collections rose from ₹7,17,922.31 crore to ₹7,55,944.96 crore. Corporate tax refunds increased from ₹1,54,724.82 crore to ₹1,69,512.18 crore, while non-corporate tax refunds increased from ₹90,126.88 crore to ₹1,38,771.22 crore. Securities Transaction Tax (STT) collections increased from ₹35,681.88 crore to ₹54,077.90 crore, with zero refunds in both periods. Gross collections under other taxes fell from ₹334.63 crore to ₹20.10 crore, while refunds rose from ₹20.51 crore to ₹23.58 crore. As a result, net collections under other taxes moved from ₹314.12 crore to minus ₹3.48 crore.
