Elaborating on the role of Primary Dealers, Jain said, "primary dealers are a vital partner for the Reserve Bank in assessing market conditions and providing valuable inputs in the runup to the announcement of the Central Government’s borrowing calendar for dated securities. The PD system in India has demonstrated resilience and has supported the Reserve Bank in the management of the overall borrowing programme of the Government in a smooth and non-disruptive manner."On the demand side, Jain pointed out that India's G-Sec market remains largely domestic and institution-driven, "on the demand side, India’s G-Sec market remains largely domestic and institution-driven. Banks, insurers, provident funds, and pension funds continue to be the principal holders of government securities. Commercial banks remain the largest holders of government securities, accounting for 35 percent of holdings as at end-March 2026, followed by insurers and provident funds. While this participation provides stability and depth to the market, the institutional capacity to absorb longer tenor issuances is relatively limited."The RBI has emphasised the need for sound fiscal and debt-management policies, a transparent and predictable bond auction calendar, better liquidity in the secondary market and greater participation from long-term institutional investors. "Recent geopolitical developments have important implications for the cost and stability of government borrowing. Going forward, a stable and resilient G-Sec market will require a more coordinated approach. India will need to continue the focus on credible fiscal and debt-management policies, a transparent and predictable auction calendar, deeper secondary-market liquidity, and broader participation from long-term institutional investors."Trading in both short-term securities (up to five years) and long-term bonds (over 15 years) accounted for only around 7 percent each in the first half of FY27. State Government Securities, meanwhile, contributed just about 5 percent to total secondary market turnover during the period. The RBI said Primary Dealers could help address these gaps by increasing market-making activity, improving access for retail investors through the Retail Direct Scheme, educating investors about government bonds and offering simpler products.
