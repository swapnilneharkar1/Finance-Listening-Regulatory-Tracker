Advt The rupee has also benefited from a slide in oil prices amid optimism over an end to the Iran war, while concerns over crowded AI-linked positions have weighed on other Asian markets.Four traders cited strong dollar demand from state-run banks near the rupee's recent highs over the past two sessions, fuelling speculation that further gains could prompt RBI dollar purchases and a return of oil-linked demand to the market.Last month the central bank had opened a dedicated dollar supply window for state-run oil refiners - alongside other measures to curb dollar demand - as the rupee threatened to breach record lows.With the currency having since recovered, traders reckon that the central bank may use the opportunity to slow the drain on foreign exchange reserves on account of the oil refiner demand and also look to replenish the kitty which has dipped to an about 3-month low of $717 billion.RATE HIKE WATCHTraders will also watch India's October inflation data due on Thursday, with headline inflation expected to approach the central bank's 6% upper tolerance limit."The composition will matter as much as the headline: evidence that food-price pressures are spilling into underlying inflation would reinforce expectations for further RBI tightening and might provide additional near-term support for INR," MUFG said in a note.
