A Reuters poll forecasts October annual inflation at 5.93%, up from 5.54% in September. Advt The RBI raised rates in October for the first time in nearly four years and shifted to a "calibrated tightening" stance, and is widely expected to raise its policy rate by 25 basis points in December.An upside surprise in inflation could spur bets on a 50-basis-point rate hike next month, traders said.Traders are closely watching headlines on the Iran war and their impact on oil prices after a Democratic sweep in US midterms raised hopes of a resolution.Oil prices have retreated from $117 per barrel before US midterms to $91 per barrel, supporting sentiment for oil-import-dependent India."Trump losing the midterms is positive for markets, but we need to see how the US-Iran war develops," said a trader at a private bank. "With oil prices easing, the RBI may not need to hike as aggressively as was feared a few weeks ago."Still, the backdrop remains fragile.The benchmark 10-year US Treasury yield neared 5% after October US inflation exceeded forecasts, prompting investors to raise bets on a larger Federal Reserve rate increase in December.New Delhi's 340-billion-rupee auction of the 10-year note on Friday will also be a key test of demand, traders said.RATESOvernight index swap rates were subdued ahead of the inflation data.The 1-year and the 2-year rate were not yet traded.
