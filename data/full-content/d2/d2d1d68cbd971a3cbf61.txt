} } Listen to This ArticleReserve Bank of India (RBI) Governor Sanjay Malhotra on Monday urged non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) to pursue responsible and sustainable credit growth, strengthen customer protection practices and bolster cybersecurity as they expand their businesses. At a meeting with managing directors and chief executives of select NBFCs, housing finance companies (HFCs), microfinance institutions and government-owned NBFCs in Mumbai, Malhotra emphasised the need for lenders to put customers first. He highlighted the importance of quality customer service, effective grievance redressal mechanisms and fair recovery practices, according to an RBI statement. The Governor also encouraged NBFCs to use the Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) for customer onboarding and ensure that customer records are updated in the registry to improve convenience. Cybersecurity was another key focus of the meeting. Malhotra urged lenders to strengthen their cybersecurity infrastructure and adopt a comprehensive, risk-based approach to managing cyber and information technology risks. He also stressed the need for boards and senior management to exercise appropriate oversight of these risks. The remarks come against the backdrop of the central bank asking banks, small finance banks (SFBs) and NBFCs to strengthen controls around application programming interfaces (APIs), tighten safeguards for third-party systems and improve monitoring of fund transfers amid a rise in cyberattacks targeting financial institutions.
