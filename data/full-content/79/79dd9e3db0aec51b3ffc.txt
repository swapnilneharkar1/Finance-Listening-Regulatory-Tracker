The bank also expects to maintain its loan yields at 8-9% while keeping deposit costs under control.The bank's repo-linked loans are reset on a T+1 basis, which management expects to result in a loan yield increase of more than 10 basis points. PNB has also said it would not raise deposit rates as liquidity is ample. These expectations were outlined in the bank's Q2FY27 conference call, as reported by Elara Capital. Advt PNB has been shedding low-yielding advances to improve yields, having reduced such loans by around Rs 55,000 crore. It has also reduced bulk deposits and certificates of deposit, which accounted for 17% of total deposits in Q2FY27, compared with 19% in the previous quarter.The bank's advances grew 14.7% year-on-year despite a Rs 23,700 crore reduction in inter-bank participation certificates exposure. PNB is targeting a retail, agriculture and MSME loan share of 58-59% by the end of FY27. Its credit pipeline stood at Rs 1.58 lakh crore.FCNR boostPNB raised $2.7 billion through FCNR(B) deposits, which it used to repay bulk deposits and certificates of deposit. Individual savings deposits grew 8.5% year-on-year, while institutional savings deposits declined 3.84%, resulting in savings account growth of around 7%.Asset quality improved in Q2FY27, with gross non-performing assets declining to 2.55% from 2.78% in the previous quarter and net NPAs falling to 0.25% from 0.28%. The overall special mention account pool stood at 2.5%, an all-time low, although the sticky MSME pool remains a monitorable.PNB expects to recover Rs 3,000-3,500 crore from written-off accounts in Q3FY27. It has lowered its expected credit loss transition impact estimate to Rs8,000 crore from the earlier Rs 9,000-10,000 crore. Management expects the ongoing ECL impact to be 15-20 basis points, resulting in credit costs of 0.2-0.3%.
