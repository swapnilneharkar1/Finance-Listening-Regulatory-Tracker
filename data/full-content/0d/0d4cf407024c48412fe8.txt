According to the announcement dated 2 November 2026, candidates may submit their feedback by email at examfeedback[at]icai[dot]in or send a letter through Speed Post to the Additional Secretary (Exams), ICAI Bhawan, Indraprastha Marg, New Delhi – 110002. The observations must reach ICAI latest by 18 November 2026. ICAI has specifically clarified that only submissions containing the candidate’s name, registration number, roll number, email ID and mobile number will be considered. Candidates should therefore ensure that all prescribed identification and contact details accompany their observations and that submissions reach the Examination Department within the stipulated deadline. Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Examination Department The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India November 2, 2026 Observations of the candidates on the question papers of CA Final Examination – November 2026 It is hereby informed that candidates can bring to the notice of the Examination Department, their observations, if any, on the question papers relating to Final Examination being held in November 2026 by e-mail at examfeedback[at]icai[dot]in or by way of a letter, sent by Speed Post, at the following address, so as to reach us latest by 18th November 2026. The Additional Secretary (Exams) The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India ICAI Bhawan Indraprastha Marg New Delhi 110 002. Please note that the observations of the candidates with Name, Registration number, Roll no., Email-id and Mobile number will only be taken into consideration.
