The project, which spans approximately three acres, offers 260 meticulously designed apartments, contributing to a total saleable area of around 0.4 million square feet. With an estimated gross development value of ₹5,500 million, Prestige Avon is set to cater to the growing demand for premium residential spaces in the region. The development features a mix of 2 and 3 BHK residences, with select layouts incorporating home office spaces to meet the evolving needs of modern urban dwellers. The apartment sizes range from approximately 1,306 to 2,160 square feet, providing ample space for both living and working. The project’s location on Thanisandra Main Road ensures excellent connectivity to major corridors such as the Outer Ring Road and Kempegowda International Airport, as well as access to established and emerging retail, commercial, and social infrastructure. Mr Irfan Razack, Chairman and Managing Director of Prestige Group, highlighted the strategic significance of the location and the thoughtful design of the residences. He remarked, “Prestige Avon is designed for homebuyers looking for a well-connected address combined with thoughtfully planned homes and contemporary lifestyle amenities. Thanisandra has emerged as one of North Bengaluru’s key residential corridors, benefiting from strong connectivity and the continued development of the region.” Prestige Group, with a legacy spanning four decades, is renowned for its diverse portfolio, which includes residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and integrated township projects across major Indian cities. As of September 2026, the group has delivered 323 projects covering 221 million square feet and has a robust pipeline of 140 projects across 240 million square feet. The launch of Prestige Avon aligns with the group’s commitment to addressing the changing expectations of urban homebuyers, particularly in the wake of increased demand for flexible living spaces. Analysts note that the project’s strategic location and comprehensive amenities are likely to attract significant interest from prospective buyers, further strengthening Prestige Estates’ position in the competitive Bengaluru real estate market.
