Both activities may now be completed up to 30 September 2027. DGFT issued the extension under Paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023, in continuation of Notification No. 31/2026-27 and Public Notice No. 27/2026-2027, both dated 20 August 2026. All other terms and conditions of Public Notice No. 27/2026-2027, as amended, continue to apply. Government of India Ministry of Commerce & Industry Department of Commerce Directorate General of Foreign Trade Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi Public Notice No. 43/2026-27-DGFT | Dated: 25th November, 2026 Subject: Extension of the timeline for conversion of imported raw sugar into white/refined sugar and its sale in the domestic market up to 30th September 2027– reg. In exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023, and in continuation of the Notification no. 31/2026-27 dated 20.08.2026 and Public Notice No. 27/2026- 2027 dated 20.08.2026, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby extends the timeline prescribed in the said Public Notice for conversion of imported raw sugar into white/refined sugar and its sale in the domestic market up to 30th September 2027. 2. All other terms and conditions of Public Notice No. 27/2026-2027 dated 20.08.2026, as amended, shall apply.
