The agency registered the case on the same day after receiving a complaint against the two officers. According to the press release, the complaint alleged that the officers had demanded ₹4 lakh from the complainant to process the audit of his company. The four-year period mentioned in the release relates to the audit. The CBI said the accused officers directed the complainant to come to the office with the demanded money on 23 November 2026. After registering the case, the agency laid a trap that day. According to the CBI, its team apprehended both officers while they were accepting ₹3 lakh from the complainant. The release therefore records two separate amounts relating to the same case. The complaint concerned an alleged demand of ₹4 lakh, and the agency reported that ₹3 lakh was accepted during the trap. The press release records the registration of the case, the trap and the apprehension of both officers, all on 23 November 2026. The CBI stated that further investigation was underway. The release gives no further details about the audit findings or the outcome of the investigation. Central Bureau of Investigation Press Releases 23-11-2026 CBI Apprehends Two CGST Superintendents in Bhubaneswar in a Bribery case The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has apprehended two CGST Superintendents in Bhubaneswar, in a bribery case. The CBI registered the instant case based on a complaint on 23.11.2026, against the Superintendents of CGST, Bhubaneswar for demanding a bribe of Rs. 4,00,000, from the complainant for processing the audit of his company, for four years. The accused persons directed the complainant to come to the office with the demanded bribe money on 23.11.2026. The CBI laid a trap on 23.11.2026 and both the Superintendents of CGST, Bhubaneswar were caught red-handed by the CBI team while accepting the bribe amount of Rs. 3,00,000 from the complainant. Further investigation is underway.
