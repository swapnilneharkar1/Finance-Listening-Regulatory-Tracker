Here are some details:* The central bank clarified that the 1 million-rupee threshold applies to each inward remittance and not the aggregate amount of receipts Advt * Non-individual service exporters receiving over 1 million rupees are required to file an export declaration form (EDF) before the credit of proceeds* Authorized banks can extend EDF filing deadlines if they consider exporters' reasons to be justified. Banks will be required to publish trade-related policies and key operating procedures on their websites* The RBI notified the original rules governing reporting requirements in January, and they went into effect on October 1* "These new regulations are designed to align with evolving global trade practices and aim to significantly enhance the ease of doing business," it said in a Friday statement($1 = 92.9300 Indian rupees)(Reporting by Jaspreet Kalra; Editing by Sonia Cheema) Reuters Published On Nov 20, 2026 at 02:14 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
