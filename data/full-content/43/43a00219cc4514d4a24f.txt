Rising funding costs and rural credit risks remain key headwinds.AI-orchestrated channels accounted for about 10% of M&M Financial Services's disbursements in the September quarter, as the non-banking finance company expands the use of artificial intelligence in lending, back-office operations and collections. Disbursements through these channels stood at Rs 1,663 crore in the second quarter of FY27, with the company reporting lower customer acquisition costs.About 75% of back-office operations are covered by AI-enabled workflows, while 12 regional vernacular AI bots support collections and customer calling. The company has reported improved operational efficiency and lower error rates from its AI initiatives, which focus on revenue growth, operations and collections, according to an Elara Capital report. Advt AI-orchestrated disbursements primarily comprise top-up loans to existing customers, refinancing of used vehicles and personal loans. The initiatives also include dealer-led customer journeys in which back-office processes have been automated.Mahindra Finance's total disbursements grew 22% year on year to Rs 16,500 crore in the quarter, led by growth across vehicle-finance categories and diversification into non-vehicle lending. Consolidated assets under management rose 15% year on year to Rs 1.56 lakh crore.Disbursements in the core vehicle-finance business grew 21% year on year, while non-vehicle disbursements increased 52%. Non-vehicle businesses accounted for 20% of consolidated lending assets under management, with vehicle finance contributing the remaining 80%. Management expects diversification to provide incremental growth beyond the core business.AUM targetThe company retained its medium-term assets under management growth target of 16% to 18% and return on assets target of 2.2% to 2.5%. Management expects future earnings growth to be driven increasingly by loan-book expansion and revenue growth, alongside incremental operating-efficiency gains.Mahindra Finance's cost-to-income ratio has declined from above 40% to below 37%. However, the company plans to deploy additional staff selectively across vehicle finance, small and medium enterprise lending and mortgage businesses, after headcount remained broadly unchanged for two years.The technology-led expansion comes amid pressure on funding costs and uncertainty over rural demand. Management reported a softer start to the festive season than a year earlier, particularly in tractor activity, while flagging drought-related risks across 282 districts in three states. It has stepped up collections in affected locations and reduced funding in some stressed areas. Advt Elara Capital retained its Accumulate rating on Mahindra Finance but reduced its March 2027 target price to Rs 350 from Rs 380. The reduction reflects a higher cost-of-equity assumption of 12.7%, against 12.1% earlier, while the brokerage retained its FY27 to FY29 earnings estimates. Other risks identified in the report include rising funding costs, weakness in the rural and agricultural economy and potential fee-income dilution from proposed insurance-commission norms.
