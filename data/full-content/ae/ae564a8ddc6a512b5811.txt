(Gauhati High Court) Summary: The Gauhati High Court considered a writ petition filed by M/s Jack and Jill, a proprietorship concern represented by its proprietor Moon Moon Brahma, and Moon Moon Brahma individually, challenging an order dated 08.08.2023 passed in Form GST REG-19 cancelling the concern’s GST registration under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. The petition was filed as WP(C)/5780/2026. On 14.10.2026, Justice Manish Choudhury heard counsel for the petitioners, the Union of India and the CGST authorities, and the State tax authorities. The Court issued notice, returnable on 06.11.2026. As all respondents were represented by their respective counsel, formal issuance of notices was dispensed with. The petitioners’ counsel was directed to serve the required extra copies of the writ petition together with annexures on the counsel representing the respondents within two working days. The order is procedural and does not decide the legality of the GST registration cancellation on merits. FULL TEXT OF THE ORDER OF GAUHATI HIGH COURT Heard Mr. C. Agarwal, learned counsel for the petitioners; Mr. P.K. Medhi, learned Central Government Counsel for the respondent no. 1; Mr. K. Jain, learned counsel on behalf of Mr. S.C. Keyal, learned Senior Counsel and Special Counsel, CGST for the respondent nos. 2 & 3; and Mr. R. Borpujari, learned counsel on behalf of Mr. B. Gogoi, learned Standing Counsel, Finance & Taxation Department for the respondent nos. 4 & 5. The petitioner has assailed an Order dated 08.08.2023 passed in Form GST Reg-19 whereby the petitioner’s registration under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 has been cancelled. Issue notice, returnable on 06.11.2026. As all the respondents are represented by their respective learned counsel, issuance of formal notices to the said respondents stand dispensed with. The learned counsel for the petitioner shall serve requisite nos. of extra copies of the writ petition along with annexures to Mr. Medhi, Mr. Jain and Mr. Borpujari within 2 [two] working days from today.
