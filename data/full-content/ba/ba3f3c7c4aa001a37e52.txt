Private insurers' APE share rose to 63.9 per cent in April-October FY27, mainly on group yearly renewable premiums. We expect healthy growth over the medium term," said Vineet Jain, Senior Director, CareEdge Ratings. Advt Individual premiums contributed only about 11 per cent, as their growth slowed to 7.9 per cent from 18.3 per cent a year earlier, when the GST exemption on individual policies lifted sales and left a high base. The slowdown was sharpest in individual non-single premiums, which rose 4.8 per cent against 19 per cent in October 2025.LIC's NBP grew 31.3 per cent YoY, against 19.5 per cent for private insurers, as its group single premiums rose 39.3 per cent.LIC accounts for 73.7 per cent of the industry's group single premiums, so its monthly growth depends heavily on their timing. Excluding group single premiums, LIC's NBP grew 11.7 per cent, against 20.5 per cent for private insurers."LIC outpaced private insurers in October because of group single premiums. Individual premiums grew 7.9 per cent in October against a base that already reflects the GST exemption on individual policies, which took effect on September 22, 2025. Industry growth should benefit from wider distribution, rising awareness and sustained demand for protection, savings and annuity products" said Saurabh Bhalerao, Director, CareEdge Ratings.However, the reliance on group and single-premium business is likely to keep monthly growth volatile, given the lumpy nature of institutional flows, Saurabh added.-IANS Advt aar/ag IANS Published On Nov 19, 2026 at 01:00 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
