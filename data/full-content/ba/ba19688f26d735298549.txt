The outperformers included IDFC FIRST Bank, YES Bank, AU Small Finance Bank, Federal Bank, Bank of Maharashtra, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India and Kotak Mahindra Bank.Adjusted net profit of the 17 banks grew 18.9% year on year, compared with 27.7% for companies in the Nifty LargeMidcap 250. However, reported net profit of the banks rose 21%, significantly higher than the broader index's 1.4% growth. The difference reflects the impact of exceptional items, labour-code costs and discontinued operations on reported earnings. Advt IDFC FIRST Bank recorded the highest adjusted profit growth among the lenders at 196.5%, although the sharp increase was partly aided by a low base in the year-ago quarter. Kotak Mahindra Bank recorded the lowest adjusted profit growth among the nine outperformers, at 29.5%. The other banks in the group included YES Bank, AU Small Finance Bank and Federal Bank among private lenders, and Bank of Maharashtra, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda and Union Bank among the other outperformers.The remaining eight banks recorded adjusted profit growth below the broader index's 27.7%. These included some of the country's largest lenders, such as HDFC Bank, State Bank of India and ICICI Bank, along with Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank, Bank of India and Indian Bank.Private sector banks shinePrivate sector banks recorded stronger adjusted profit growth overall than their public sector counterparts. Adjusted net profit at private lenders increased 23.4% year on year, compared with 14.4% at public sector banks. The difference was partly attributable to State Bank of India's exceptional gains, which lifted its reported profit growth to 14.3%, while adjusted profit increased just 1.2%.SBI remained the largest contributor to aggregate profits, accounting for 21.8% of the combined net profit of the 17 lenders. Its reported net profit rose to Rs 23,038 crore, while adjusted profit stood at Rs 15,749 crore. Gains from the sale of stakes in the National Stock Exchange and SBI Funds Management accounted for much of the difference. Advt Net interest income across the 17 banks increased 14.7% year on year, with public sector banks reporting growth of 15.3%, marginally ahead of the 14.1% recorded by private lenders. Aggregate provisions and contingencies declined 20.3%, providing support to profitability, although provisions for non-performing assets increased 0.7%.The banks also performed better than brokerage estimates, with aggregate reported net profit exceeding expectations by 4.8% and net interest income beating estimates by 2.1%. Fifteen of the 17 lenders reported profits above estimates, while 15 also exceeded net interest income expectations.
