And Another (Allahabad High Court) Summary: The Allahabad High Court, Lucknow Bench, in MakeMyTrip India Private Limited Vs State of U.P. and Another, quashed a GST show cause notice issued in the name of RedBus India Private Limited after its merger with MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited. The petitioner approached the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, challenging the notice dated 03.08.2026 bearing Reference No. ZD090826012071M, issued in Form GST DRC-01, along with the accompanying communication titled “Notice for framing order in remanded cases”. The petitioner sought cancellation of the notice, a declaration that the proceedings initiated pursuant to it were void ab initio, and interim protection against further proceedings. The dispute arose in connection with proceedings under Section 73 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, relating to FY 2021-22. The Court recorded that RedBus India Private Limited had merged with MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited with effect from 01.02.2026. The petitioner had communicated this development to the Department on 12.06.2026 through its submission in response to an earlier show cause notice under Section 73 of the CGST Act. Notwithstanding this communication, the Department issued a fresh show cause notice on 03.08.2026 in the name of RedBus India Private Limited. The petitioner contended that, following the merger, the notice ought to have been issued in the name of the amalgamated company, MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited, rather than the erstwhile company. After considering the factual position and the petitioner’s submissions, the Division Bench comprising Justice Shekhar B. Saraf and Justice Amitabh Kumar Rai expressly agreed with the contention that the notice should have been issued to the amalgamated company. Consequently, the High Court quashed and set aside the show cause notice dated 03.08.2026. However, the Court granted liberty to the Department to proceed in accordance with law and disposed of the writ petition. The decision therefore invalidated the particular notice issued in the name of RedBus after the merger, without deciding the underlying GST liability or permanently preventing lawful proceedings against the appropriate entity. FULL TEXT OF THE JUDGMENT/ORDER OF ALLAHABAD HIGH COURT 1. Heard Sri Suyash Agrawal, Advocate assisted by Ms. Surangama Sharma, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner and Sri Sanjay Sarin, learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel appearing on behalf of the State. 2. This is a writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, wherein the writ petitioner has made the following main prayers:- “a. To issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of Certiorari under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, 1950, to quash and set aside the Impugned Notice bearing Reference No. ZD090826012071M dated 03.08.2026, issued by Respondent No. 2 in Form GST DRC-01, along with the accompanying communication of even date titled “Notice for framing order in remanded cases”; (Annexure No. 1) and b. To hold and declare the proceedings initiated pursuant to the Impugned Notice dated 03.08.2026 to be void ab initio; and c. Pending disposal of the present Writ Petition, to stay all further proceedings, including the passing of any order, pursuant to the Impugned Notice dated 03.08.2026.” 3. Upon a perusal of the factual matrix, it appears that RedBus India Private Limited merged with M/s MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited with effect from February 1, 2026. This information was communicated to the Department on June 12, 2026, through a submission made by the petitioner in response to the show cause notice issued under Section 73 of the CGST Act, 2017, for the financial year 2021–2022. Subsequently, a fresh show cause notice dated August 3, 2026, was issued in the name of RedBus India Private Limited. 4. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner submits that once the merger had taken place, the show cause notice should have been issued in the name of the amalgamated company, namely, M/s MakeMyTrip (India) Private Limited. 5. We are at consensus ad idem with the submission advanced by learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner. 6. In light of the same, the impugned show cause notice dated August 3, 2026 is quashed and set aside with liberty granted to the Department to proceed in accordance with law. 7. With the above directions, the writ petition is disposed of.
