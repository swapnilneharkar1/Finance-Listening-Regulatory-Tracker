The dispute centres on claims over the family's ancestral wealth, including promoter stakes in listed companies such as Bharat Forge Ltd, Kalyani Steels Ltd and Hikal Ltd, besides real estate, land, jewellery and other assets. One of the principal disputes concerns an alleged family arrangement of 1993-94 between Baba Kalyani and his father, Neelkanth Kalyani. Hiremath claims the arrangement required the transfer of shares in Hikal held through Kalyani family-controlled entities to her and her family. Baba Kalyani has disputed the existence of a binding agreement, maintaining that the documents do not establish an enforceable obligation to transfer the shares. In a suit filed before the Bombay High Court in 2023, Hiremath and her husband, Jaidev Hiremath, sought enforcement of the alleged family arrangement. The Hiremaths claim that implementation would increase their stake in Hikal to nearly 68.85 per cent, giving them control of the pharmaceutical and lifesciences company. The dispute has also widened into litigation over the alleged Kalyani family HUF, or Hindu Undivided Family. Hiremath's children, Sameer and Pallavi, have separately sought partition of the family's assets, claiming coparcenary rights. The Kalyani side has denied that the businesses and properties constitute joint family assets. Several connected civil, partition and probate proceedings are pending before courts in Mumbai, Pune, Wai and Karad.
