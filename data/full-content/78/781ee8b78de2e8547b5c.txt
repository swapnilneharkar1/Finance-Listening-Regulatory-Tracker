The press note continues Office Memorandum No. 1(1)/2013-LC dated 13 March 2013 and was issued with the approval of the competent authority. DPIIT is the licensing authority for manufacturers located outside Special Economic Zones (SEZs). The Department of Commerce (DoC) is the licensing authority for manufacturers located within SEZs and for Export Oriented Units (EOUs). The press note identifies EOUs separately from manufacturers within SEZs, so a manufacturer identifying the competent authority should consider its EOU status as well as its location. Both assignments concern industrial licences for defence purposes under the 1951 Act. The Industrial Licensing Section issued the press note under file reference No. 7(3)/2009-IP Vol-IV. The circulation list includes the Department of Commerce, the Department of Defence Production and the Ministry of Home Affairs. It also asks the Press Information Bureau to give the press note publicity, NIC at DPIIT to upload it to the department’s website, and DPIIT’s Hindi Section to provide the Hindi version. Government of India Ministry of Commerce and Industry Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade Industrial Licensing Section *** Press Note No. 4 (2026 Series) In continuation to the Office Memorandum No. 1(1)/2013-LC dated 13.03.2013 issued by the Department, it is reiterated that the following agencies are competent to issue Industrial Licenses for Defence purposes under the I(D&R) Act 1951: a. Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) for manufacturers located outside Special Economic Zones (SEZs) b. Department of Commerce (DoC) for manufacturers located within Special Economic Zones (SEZs) as well as for Export Oriented Units (EOUs). 2. This issues with the approval of the competent authority.
