Bullion briefly fell below the key $3,500 level on Wednesday for the first time since September 2025.US gold futures for December delivery gained 1.2% to $3,578.70."Investors are covering shorts going into a major US holiday, which is driving the price action in gold today," said Ilya Spivak, head of global macro at Tastylive."Gold continues to be sold to raise cash in this sentiment sensitive environment amid risk aversion," he added. Advt The US economy grew much faster than initially estimated in the third quarter, while inflation increased further in October, reinforcing expectations that the Fed will raise rates next month and again in early 2027.The US central bank last raised interest rates in September.A Fed survey showed that economic growth persisted at a "slight to moderate" pace across most of the US in recent weeks, while inflation remained sticky and employment levels rose.Higher interest rates increase the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding bullion, reducing its appeal to investors.Asian shares meanwhile dug out a rare rally after Wall Street fought back from deep lows, though concerns over the circular financing of AI and a new round of US tariffs made for a muted mood in a holiday-thinned session.
