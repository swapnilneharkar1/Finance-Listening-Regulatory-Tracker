Summary : AU Small Finance Bank Limited’s published retail fixed-deposit schedule lists domestic, NRE and NRO deposits below ₹3 crore, with callable resident deposits available across terms from seven days to 120 months. The rate table checked on 10 November 2026 states that the general domestic retail rates are effective from 1 September 2026. For the 30-month-one-day to 36-month slab, the published rate is 7.40% per annum and the annualised yield is 7.61%. The separate senior-citizen table, stated to be effective from 10 June 2026, shows 7.90% and an annualised yield of 8.14% for the same slab, restricted to eligible resident Indian individuals aged sixty or more who have a banking relationship with the bank. Callable deposits permit premature withdrawal, but the bank applies the lower of the contracted-tenure base rate and the rate for the actual period held, followed by the current 1% premature-closure penalty. Domestic deposits closed within seven days earn no interest. From 1 April 2026, resident bank-interest withholding falls under section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, with thresholds of ₹50,000 for a person other than a senior citizen and ₹1,00,000 for a senior citizen. DICGC cover aggregates principal and interest up to ₹5 lakh in the same right and capacity across all branches of the bank. Callable Means Liquidity With a Formula, Not Penalty-Free Exit The bank distinguishes callable and non-callable deposits. A callable fixed deposit may be withdrawn before the contracted maturity, subject to the bank’s rules and any lien. On premature closure, the bank first identifies the lower of two base rates: the base rate for the original contracted tenure, and the base rate that applied on the booking date to the period for which the deposit actually remained. It then applies the premature-closure penalty, currently 1% for retail deposits, including sweep-in and partial closure. Why the booked headline rate may not determine the exit return Suppose a depositor books a deposit in the 30-month-one-day to 36-month slab but closes it after a shorter period. The bank does not simply subtract one percentage point from 7.40%. It compares the relevant base rates under its published formula using the booking-date schedule, chooses the lower base and then applies the penalty. The exact result depends on the original receipt, actual completed tenure, the applicable historical rate card, customer category and bank computation. A depositor should request the premature value before authorising closure. For a domestic fixed deposit withdrawn within seven days, the bank states that no interest is paid. A tax-saving fixed deposit is different: the official page states that it has a sixty-month tenure, and the support page says it cannot be prematurely withdrawn. This article concerns the ordinary callable resident deposit, not the non-callable or tax-saving variants. Senior-Citizen Rate Is Eligibility-Based The published senior table is limited to resident Indian individual senior citizens aged sixty or above who have a banking relationship with AU Small Finance Bank. The special rate is for resident deposits. The first holder and account constitution can affect eligibility, and an NRE or NRO deposit should not be assumed to receive the resident senior benefit. Identity and date-of-birth data should be correct before booking because later reclassification is not automatic. Tax Treatment From 1 April 2026 Fixed-deposit interest is income and the bank’s withholding does not determine final tax liability. For a sum credited or paid on or after 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies. Section 393(1), Table item 5(ii), sets the resident bank-interest threshold at ₹1,00,000 for a senior citizen and ₹50,000 for a person other than a senior citizen. Once the aggregate covered interest exceeds the threshold, tax is deducted at the applicable rate under the statutory provisions. Interest across deposits with the bank must be aggregated as required; splitting principal into several receipts does not create a fresh threshold for each receipt. TDS is a collection mechanism. Interest remains part of taxable income under the applicable computation rules even when no tax is deducted because the threshold is not crossed or a valid declaration applies. The depositor should reconcile the interest certificate, Form 26AS or annual information statement, books and return. Senior citizens should separately examine any deduction available for eligible deposit interest under the law and regime applicable to them; the TDS threshold and an income deduction are different concepts. Declarations and PAN require current-year verification A declaration seeking non-deduction can be used only when the statutory eligibility conditions are satisfied and the current prescribed form is correctly furnished. It is not an exemption certificate for every depositor. A missing or invalid PAN can change the withholding result. The bank’s record should be updated well before interest credit, and any lower or nil deduction position should be supported by the current Income-tax Rules, 2026 and portal process. DICGC Protects an Aggregate, Not Every Receipt Separately AU Small Finance Bank Limited appears in the Reserve Bank of India list of scheduled small finance banks, and DICGC’s guide states that deposits with insured banks, including fixed deposits, are covered. The ceiling is ₹5 lakh for principal and accrued interest together for each depositor in the same right and same capacity. Balances across savings, current, recurring and fixed deposits at all branches of the same bank are aggregated. Several receipts or branches do not multiply the cover. Different rights and capacities can receive separate treatment under DICGC rules, but names, order of joint holders and legal capacity must genuinely differ in the manner recognised by the scheme. Deposit insurance is not a guarantee of rate, liquidity on demand, immediate access in every stress event or protection beyond the statutory limit. A maturity amount above ₹5 lakh does not become fully insured merely because the original principal was below that amount. Booking and Review File Save the official rate page or bank rate card applicable on the booking date. Confirm callable status, amount category, tenure slab, customer category and payout option on the receipt. Record nomination, operating instructions and maturity or renewal instructions. Track aggregate annual interest for tax and withholding rather than receipt by receipt. Aggregate all same-right and same-capacity balances when assessing DICGC exposure. Before premature closure, obtain the bank’s value and rate calculation in writing or through the authenticated channel.
