Issued under Section 400(1) read with Schedule VI [Table: S.No. 11] of the Income-tax Act, 2025, the notification applies to dividend income paid by an IFSC unit primarily engaged in aircraft leasing to a company that is also an IFSC unit primarily engaged in aircraft leasing. The exemption from TDS under Section 393(1) [Table: S.No. 7] is subject to the payee furnishing a duly verified statement-cum-declaration in Form No. 1(N), identifying the relevant tax year and confirming eligibility for exemption under Schedule VI. The payer must not deduct tax on dividend payments made or credited after receiving the prescribed declaration from the payee. However, the payer must report all such payments on which tax has not been deducted in the applicable TDS statement under Section 397(3)(b), read with Rule 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. Form No. 1(N) requires details including the payee’s name, PAN, IFSC unit particulars, contact information and tax year, together with declarations confirming the continuation of aircraft leasing activities and eligibility for exemption. The notification also authorises the income-tax systems authorities to prescribe procedures for secure data capture, transmission, document uploading and record management. The terms aircraft, International Financial Services Centre and Unit are defined by reference to the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005. The notification is deemed effective retrospectively from 1 April 2026, with an explanatory memorandum certifying that no person is adversely affected by its retrospective operation. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) (CENTRAL BOARD OF DIRECT TAXES) Notification No. 141/2026-Income Tax | Dated: 4th November, 2026 S.O. 6016(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 400(1) read with Schedule VI [Table: S.No. 11] of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby specifies that no deduction of tax shall be made under section 393(1)[Table: S.No. 7] of the said Act from any income in the nature of dividend paid by any unit of an International Financial Services Centre, primarily engaged in the business of leasing of an aircraft (hereinafter referred to as the payer) to a company, being a Unit of an International Financial Services Centre primarily engaged in the business of leasing of an aircraft (hereinafter referred to as the payee) subject to the following, namely:- 1. (1) The payee shall,— a. furnish a statement-cum-declaration in the Form No.1(N) annexed to this notification (hereinafter referred to as the said Form) to the payer giving details of the tax year in which the dividend income eligible for exemption under Schedule VI [Table: S.No. 11] of the said Act is payable; and b. such statement-cum-declaration shall be furnished and verified in the manner specified in the said Form for the tax year in which the dividend income eligible for exemption under Schedule VI [Table: S.No. 11] of the said Act is payable; (2) The payer shall,— a. not deduct tax on payment made or credited to the recipient of such dividend (payee) after the date of receipt of copy of statement-cum-declaration in the said Form from payee; and b. furnish the particulars of all the payments made to the recipient of such dividend on which tax has not been deducted in view of this Notification in the statement of deduction of tax referred to in section 397(3)(b) of the said Act, read with the rule 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. 2. The Principal Director General of Income-tax (Systems) or the Director General of Income-tax (Systems), as the case may be, shall lay down procedures, formats and standards for ensuring secure capture and transmission of data and uploading of documents and they shall also be responsible for evolving and implementing appropriate security, archival and retrieval policies. Explanation.— For the purpose of this notification,— i. “aircraft” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in Note 3 of Schedule VI to the said Act; ii. “International Financial Services Centre” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause (q) of section 2 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005); and iii. “Unit” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause 2(zc) of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005). 3. This notification shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of April, 2026.
