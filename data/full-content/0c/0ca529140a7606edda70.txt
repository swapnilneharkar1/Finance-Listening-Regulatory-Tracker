Articles 1 min read Rupee falls 6 paise to 93.77 against US dollar in early trade In early trading, the rupee fell by 6 paise against the US dollar, settling at 93.77. The rise of the dollar stems from strong US inflation and employment data that have pushed Treasury yields higher. However, falling crude oil prices and robust domestic equity markets offered some support to the rupee. Additionally, foreign institutional investors acquired equities worth Rs 12,563.33 crore on Wednesday, while the dollar index increased by 0. Advt "The rupee's underlying momentum remains positive, backed by a steady return of foreign capital and softer crude prices. If these supportive factors continue, USD/INR could extend its decline towards 93.50, with 93.00 emerging as the next important level to watch. However, any further strengthening in the dollar index could put renewed pressure on the rupee, pushing USD/INR towards the 94.10-94.30 resistance zone," CR Forex Advisors MD Amit Pabari said.Meanwhile, the dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength against a basket of six currencies, was trading at 103.03, higher by 0.12 per cent.The dollar strengthened on hotter-than-expected CPI inflation in the US, raising odds of a rate hike by the Fed in December.Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading 1.10 per cent lower at USD 91.74 per barrel in futures trade.On the domestic equity market front, Sensex advanced 166.44 points to 82,013.58 in early trade; Nifty was trading 55.65 points higher at 25,316.55.Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) purchased equities worth Rs 12,563.33 crore on Wednesday, according to exchange data.
