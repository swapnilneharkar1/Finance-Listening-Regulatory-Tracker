Articles 3 min read IOB net profit up 56 pc at Rs 1,962.67 cr in September quarter, bank plans to launch QIP in Q4 PTI Published On Oct 26, 2026 at 07:29 PM IST var news_msid = 135213568; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai, State-owned lender Indian Overseas Bank on Monday reported a 55.9 per cent jump in consolidated net profit to Rs 1,962.67 crore for the September quarter, driven by core income growth and improved asset quality.In the year-ago period, the bank had reported a net profit of Rs 1,258.82 crore.Its core net interest income (NII) increased 26.41 per cent year-on-year to Rs 3,867 crore in the second quarter of the current fiscal, from Rs 3,059 crore in the year-ago period.Domestic net interest margins (NIM) of the bank improved to 3.49 per cent as on September 30, 2026, from 3.48 per cent as on June 30, 2026, and 3.35 per cent as on September 30, 2025, the investor presentation showed. Advt The advances surged 24.35 per cent during the quarter compared to the year-ago period.Non-interest income of the bank declined by 10.11 per cent year-on-year to Rs 1,227 crore in Q2FY27, from Rs 1,365 crore in Q2FY26, due to a drop in recovery from technically written-off accounts."The major reduction was due to the absence of income from priority sector lending certificates (PSLCs). We earned around Rs 900 crore in commission from PSLCs in the first quarter, but there was no such income in the second quarter. This was the primary reason for the decline," Ajay Kumar Srivastava, managing director and chief executive officer of Indian Overseas Bank, told PTI.In the quarter under review, the bank's total deposits increased by 20.16 per cent YoY to Rs 4.07 lakh crore, from Rs 3.39 lakh crore a year ago.The sharp growth in total deposits was due to a 20.73 per cent growth in current and savings accounts (CASA).On a standalone basis, the public lender reported a 56 per cent rise in net profit to Rs 1,912 crore in Q2 FY27, compared to Rs 1,226 crore in the year-ago period.Standalone interest income of the bank rose to Rs 9,201 crore during the quarter, up from Rs 7,849 crore in the corresponding quarter of the preceding fiscal.The lender mobilised USD 1.12 billion, equivalent to around Rs 11,000 crore, under the FCNR(B) concessional swap. Advt The deposits came from a mix of domestic and overseas branches, with around 70 per cent sourced from overseas branches and the remaining 30 per cent from domestic branches, Srivastava said."We have used only one-time leverage. We did not opt for five, nine or 15 times leverage. The leverage is around 70-80 per cent of the total deposits mobilised, while the remainder is unleveraged," Srivastava said.The bank has not seen any impact of El Nino on its portfolio so far, whether in retail, agriculture or MSME lending, he added.The retail, agriculture and MSME (RAM) advances segment grew 34.51 per cent YoY to Rs 2.83 lakh crore in Q2FY27, from Rs 2.10 lakh crore a year ago. Advt "Our RAM portfolio has already grown by around 35 per cent year-on-year, and we expect some further growth. Demand for retail, housing and vehicle loans has been healthy, and we expect this momentum to continue through the busy season," Srivastava said.The gross non-performing asset (NPA) ratio of the bank improved to 1.19 per cent in the September quarter, from 1.33 per cent in the previous quarter and 1.83 per cent in the corresponding period a year ago.Srivastava said the bank has an approval to raise Rs 5,000 crore through a qualified institutional placement (QIP) in the current financial year and has obtained almost all the necessary approvals. Advt "We plan to enter the market by the end of the current quarter or possibly at the beginning of the fourth quarter, depending on market conditions," he added.He said that if the bank raised the entire Rs 5,000 crore, the dilution would be around 6 per cent. The Government of India's shareholding, currently at 92.44 per cent, would decline to around 86 per cent.Shares of Indian Overseas Bank settled at Rs 30.34 apiece, up 5.71 per cent, on the BSE. SHMRevenue also grew 11.6 per cent quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) from ₹5,527.7 crore in the preceding quarter.
