Its circular dated 25 November 2026 keeps the list in Annexure-A to the circular of 5 May 2026 unchanged. Under the earlier circular, a benchmark or index qualifies as significant if the daily average cumulative assets under management (AUM) tracking it across mutual fund schemes exceed ₹20,000 crore in each of the preceding six months. The six-month periods end on 30 June and 31 December each year. In the January–June review, BSE 200 and Nifty CPSE, listed at serial numbers 2 and 33 respectively in Annexure-A, failed to satisfy the threshold prescribed by paragraph 4 of the May circular. SEBI retained both indices under paragraph 5 of that circular. The review circular took effect immediately and leaves all other provisions of the May circular unchanged. SEBI issued it under Section 11(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, read with Regulations 38 and 2(1)(u) of the SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024. The circular is addressed to index providers, stock exchanges, mutual funds, asset management companies and the Association of Mutual Funds in India. Securities and Exchange Board of India Circular No.: HO/47/17/12(53)2026-MRD-POD5/I/26407/2026 |Dated: November 25, 2026 To, All Index Providers All Stock Exchanges All Mutual Funds All Asset Management Companies Association of Mutual Funds in India Sir/Madam, Subject: Half-yearly review of ‘Significant Indices’ under SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024 1. SEBI, vide Circular No. HO/47/17/12(8)2025-MRD-POD2 dated May 05, 2026 (hereinafter “the said circular”), specified that a Benchmark or Index shall be considered as ‘Significant Indices’ if the daily average cumulative Asset Under Management (AUM) tracking the Benchmark or Index across schemes of Mutual Fund(s) exceeds ₹20,000 Crore for each of the past six months, ending on 30th June and 31st December each year. 2. In terms of the aforesaid provisions, the half-yearly review of the list of ‘Significant Indices’ has been undertaken for the period from January 01, 2026 to June 30, 2026. Accordingly, the list of Significant Indices specified in the Annexure-A of the said circular is unchanged. 3. In the half-yearly review for the period from January 01, 2026 to June 30, 2026, the indices at Sr. No. 2 viz. BSE 200 and Sr. No. 33 viz. Nifty CPSE of Annexure-A of the said circular, did not satisfy the threshold limit for ‘Significant Indices’ in terms of Para 4 of the said circular. However, these indices are being retained in the list in terms of Para 5 of the said circular. 4. All other provisions of the said circular shall remain unchanged. 5. The provisions of this circular shall come into force with immediate effect. 6. This circular is being issued in exercise of powers conferred under Section 11(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 read with Regulation 38 and Regulation 2(1)(u) of SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024 to protect the interest of investors in securities and to promote development of, and to regulate securities market. 7. This circular is available on SEBI website at www.sebi.gov.in at “Legal Framework – Circulars.” 8. This circular has been issued with the approval of the competent authority.
