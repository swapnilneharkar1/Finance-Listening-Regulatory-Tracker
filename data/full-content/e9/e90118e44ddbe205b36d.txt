Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Summary: The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), through Circular No. HO/17/11/17(7)2026-DDHS-POD2/I/24929/2026 dated 2 November 2026, has reduced the cooling-off period for Offer for Sale (OFS) transactions involving illiquid private listed Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) to ±8 weeks. The amendment modifies paragraph 19.2 of Chapter 1 of SEBI’s Master Circular dated 30 December 2024 and follows feedback from market participants aimed at facilitating ease of doing business. The OFS framework applicable to listed equity shares continues to apply to REIT and InvIT units, subject to specified conditions. For InvITs, the trading lot must match the secondary-market trading lot prescribed under the SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014. Retail investor provisions do not apply to OFS transactions involving private listed InvITs, and such offers remain open for only one trading day (T day). The circular takes immediate effect and requires recognised stock exchanges to implement necessary system changes, amend applicable rules and bye-laws, and disseminate the revised requirements to market participants and investors. Securities and Exchange Board of India Circular No. HO/17/11/17(7)2026-DDHS-POD2/I/24929/2026 | Dated: November 02, 2026 To, Bharat InvITs Association All Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) All Parties to InvITs All Recognized Stock Exchanges All Registered Depositories All Recognized Clearing Corporations Madam / Sir, Subject: Cooling off period for offer for sale by Private listed InvITs 1. SEBI vide Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024 and circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2023/134 dated August 03, 2023 specified the comprehensive framework on Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares including units of REITs and InvITs through stock exchange mechanism. 2. Based on the feedback received from market participants and in order to facilitate ease of doing business, it has been decided to reduce the cooling off period for illiquid private listed InvITs. Accordingly, Paragraph 19.2 under Chapter 1 of SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, has been modified as under: “19.2 OFS Framework for sale of units of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) The OFS framework prescribed at paragraph 19.1 above for equity shares of listed companies shall also be applicable for OFS of units of REITs and InvITs subject to the following – i. In case of OFS for InvITs, the trading lot shall be same as the trading lot in the secondary market as prescribed in SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014 ii. The provisions related to retail investors specified in paragraph 19.1 above shall not be applicable in case of OFS for private listed InvITs and such OFS shall remain open only for one day (i.e. T day) iii. The cooling off period in case of private listed InvITs whose units are illiquid shall be ±8 weeks” 3. This circular shall come into force with immediate effect. 4. All recognized stock exchanges are advised to: a. take necessary steps and put in place necessary systems for implementation of the above. b. make necessary amendments to the relevant bye-laws, rules and regulations, wherever required, for the implementation of the above; and c. bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of market participants (including investors) and also to disseminate the same on their website; 5. This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 to protect the interest of investors in securities and to promote the development of and to regulate the securities market. 6. This Circular is available on the website of the Securities and Exchange Board of India at www.sebi.gov.in under the category “Legal” and under the drop down “Circulars”.
