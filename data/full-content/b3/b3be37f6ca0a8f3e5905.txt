The extension applies to raw sugar classified under Exim Code 170114 in Chapter 17 of Schedule I (Import Policy) of ITC (HS), 2022. The revised policy condition continues to classify imports under this Exim code as “Free” subject to a duty-free TRQ of 10 lakh metric tonnes. The earlier policy condition applied the same quota through 31 December 2026. The amended condition carries that quota through 31 March 2027, subject to the applicable conditions. All other terms and conditions of the notification dated 20 August 2026, as amended from time to time, continue to apply. The Central Government issued the amendment under Sections 3 and 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992, read with paragraphs 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023. It follows the notifications dated 20 August 2026 and 19 October 2026. The latter had set the previous deadline of 31 December 2026. The November notification was issued with the approval of the Minister of Commerce and Industry. S.O. (E): In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 and Section 5 of Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992, read with paragraph 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time to time, and in continuation of Notification No. 31/2025-26 dated 20.08.2026 and Notification No. 45/2025-26 dated 19.10.2026 , the Central Government hereby amends the import policy condition of ‘Raw Sugar’ covered under Exim 170114 of ITC HS, 2022, Schedule -I (Import Policy) as under: Exim Code Item Description Existing Policy Existing Policy Condition Revised Policy Condition 170114 Raw Sugar Free Import is “Free” subject to a Tariff Rate Quota (TRQ) of 10 lakh MT (duty-free) up to 31.12.2026, subject to the conditions specified below. Import is “Free” subject to a Tariff Rate Quota (TRQ) of 10 lakh MT (duty-free) up to 31.03.2027, subject to the conditions specified below. 2. All other terms and conditions specified in the Notification No. 31/2025-26 dated 20.08.2026 as amended from time to time, shall remain unchanged. Effect of the Notification: The validity/timeline for duty-free import of 10 lakh MT of raw sugar under the TRQ is extended from 31.12.2026 to 31.03.2027. This is issued with the approval of the Hon’ble Minister of Commerce & Industry.
